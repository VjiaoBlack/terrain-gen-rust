This also enables the wood skill to grow above pure-decay value.
3. **Fix tick_config event modifier chain** (`game/mod.rs:2135-2148`): Thread TickConfig through `step_water_cycle()` so drought/harvest modifiers have effect. Currently Pillar 2 is undermined by structurally impossible drought events.
